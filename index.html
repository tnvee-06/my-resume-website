REEL 07 · FEATURE

(07) The details behind desire

A personal edit of the things I notice, and want to understand: why some names, stores and objects feel worth so much more than what they're made of.

curiosities, not credentials

01

The House Behind the Name

What makes a name worth remembering for a hundred years?

Some brands carry generations of history with them. I'm fascinated by how a house builds and protects its identity, through its heritage, its codes and the stories it tells about itself, while staying relevant enough to be wanted today.

02

The Experience of Premium Retail

How does a room tell you what a brand is worth?

A luxury store is rarely just a shop. The light, the pace, the presentation, the way you're greeted: space, service and storytelling shape how you feel about a brand before you've touched a single thing. I want to understand how those choices are made.

03

Where Craft Meets the Customer

Why do we pay for what we can't see?

Indian and Western craft traditions hold skills passed down over generations. Craft is real, but its value also lives in the customer's mind. I'm curious about where the two meet: how craftsmanship and consumer psychology together shape perceived value.
