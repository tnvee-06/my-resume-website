Committee Rooms & Business Blueprints

Model United Nations

Model United Nations

4 conferences

Across four Model United Nations conferences, I learned to step into perspectives that weren't my own. Each one meant representing an assigned country, researching the topics on the agenda, and understanding where other delegations stood before I spoke.

In committee, I learned to build an argument, defend it in formal debate, and listen closely enough to respond well. It sharpened my research, public speaking and critical thinking, and the habit of looking at an issue from more than one side.

  • Best Delegate · 2025
  • Best Reporter · 2026

Blueprint · YEB-2025

Young Entrepreneurs Bootcamp

BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus · 2025

The Young Entrepreneurs Bootcamp showed me a more practical, hands-on side of entrepreneurship. We were introduced to the Rakesh Kapoor Innovation Center (RKIC), with its CNC machines, 3D printers and robotics sandboxes: spaces built for hardware experimentation, where ideas get made, tested and taken apart again.

The programme also explored AI prompt engineering, and how a physical concept can be translated into code and, eventually, into an investor-ready pitch.

What stayed with me was the contrast: physical innovation on one side, digital thinking on the other, and entrepreneurship as the bridge connecting technology, creativity, experimentation and communication.

CH. 07 · THE FEATUREluxury, craft & the stories behind objects

The Details Behind Desire

Why does a piece of jewellery become a conversation? Why can an unexpected shape become iconic? And how does a fashion house turn imagination into an identity people recognise?

Fig. 01 · A surrealist study. Original illustration.

Cover story

The Surreal Signature: Schiaparelli

Schiaparelli is the house I keep returning to. Its surrealist jewellery treats an earring or a brooch less like an accessory and more like an idea: symbolism, art and imagination, given a shape and finished with craftsmanship.

What fascinates me isn't only how striking the pieces are, but how consistently they say the same thing. A surreal detail becomes a signature, and a signature becomes an identity people recognise instantly. I want to understand how that happens: how a house protects something so imaginative, presents it, and keeps it desirable over time.

  1. Why do some objects become iconic?
  2. How do heritage and innovation coexist?
  3. How does craftsmanship shape perceived value?
  4. How do premium brands stay desirable over time?
